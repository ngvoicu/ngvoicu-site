About

I started out doing web design and print layout. In 2016 I began working as a Java developer, and I’ve built enterprise software ever since. Along the way I added Kotlin, Python, TypeScript and now Rust.

Over time, the part I came to care about most was security: who is allowed to do what, and how you prove it. That’s where my enterprise work has moved since 2021.

When generative AI arrived, I taught myself through a lot of trial and error. Then I was given the role of driving my department’s move to AI-native work. Now I build agents that change real code and call real services, and I design their limits first.

Outside my job I build products of my own and open-source tools for coding agents.

Teaching and mentoring

I taught Java in a classroom in 2020, mostly through live coding, so students learned by doing. I’ve recorded screencasts since 2016 and written articles since 2019, and these days I run workshops on working with AI agents.

At work, I like being someone colleagues can come to when they’re stuck.

  • I mentored one colleague for five years, from new hire to senior engineer. Today we build together as close technical partners.
  • I helped an intern become a permanent engineer while we delivered a data migration together.
  • I’ve helped interns deploy their first application to our Kubernetes platform, and colleagues take their first steps in a new language.
  • I sit on hiring panels for engineering roles.
  • When something needs fixing, I pull a few people into a small team, keep us on the problem and work through it with them.
