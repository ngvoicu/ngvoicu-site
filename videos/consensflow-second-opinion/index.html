ConsensFlow: a second AI opinion inside Claude Code & Pi

By Gabriel Voicu, 21 July 2026

Video, 3:26

ConsensFlow has since become a desktop app that runs a team of AI coding agents on your project: a Chief of Staff plans the work on a board, and its workers, advisors, reviewers and testers take the tasks in their own terminals of Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Pi or Devin. See consensflow.ngvoicu.dev for the current app.

This video shows the plugin it grew from, and is kept here as history rather than as instructions.

A Claude Code plugin and a Pi extension that let the AI you were coding with ask a different AI to review a spec or a diff, or take the easy work. Real captures of Fable 5 and GLM 5.2 finding the same bug independently.
