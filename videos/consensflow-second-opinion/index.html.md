# ConsensFlow: a second AI opinion inside Claude Code & Pi

By Gabriel Voicu, 21 July 2026

Video, 3:26

ConsensFlow has since become a desktop app that runs a team of AI coding agents on your project: a Chief of Staff plans the work on a board, and its workers, advisors, reviewers and testers take the tasks in their own terminals of Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Pi or Devin. See [consensflow.ngvoicu.dev](https://consensflow.ngvoicu.dev) for the current app.

This video shows the plugin it grew from, and is kept here as history rather than as instructions.

[ConsensFlow: a second AI opinion inside Claude Code & Pi](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iKuBFEDE8Go)

A Claude Code plugin and a Pi extension that let the AI you were coding with ask a **different** AI to review a spec or a diff, or take the easy work. Real captures of Fable 5 and GLM 5.2 finding the same bug independently.
